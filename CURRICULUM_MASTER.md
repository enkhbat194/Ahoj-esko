# AHOJ — Curriculum master v1 (working policy)
2026-11-25. Authoritative design constraints; individual Czech content remains draft until verified.
## Goal
Mongolian-speaking adults: Pre-A1 to B2, early meaningful listening and speaking, usable in Czech daily life. CEFR is an outcome reference, not a prescribed word list or course length. A0 is a foundation label. No arbitrary promise of B2 from a fixed number of lessons.
## Learning model
Outcome → scenario → necessary lexical units and surface forms → grammar/phonology prerequisites → learning tasks → unaided spoken transfer → delayed recall. Maintain distinct IDs for lemma, sense/POS, form, phrase/sentence, audio and skill. Relations can cycle; prerequisites cannot. A known lemma does not imply its inflection is known. A fixed phrase may be taught as a chunk before analysis. Every assessed item/distractor must have been introduced, or be explicitly scaffolded.
- Select words by practical utility, recombination, level, spoken frequency and prerequisites. Corpus rank and teaching order are independent. Use CNC, KorpusDB, MorfFlex/MorphoDiTa and VALLEX; validate sense, case and actual use. Never invent ranks or claim authored examples are statistically measured collocations.
- Microcycle: introduce 2–3 useful forms/chunks individually or together; listen, imitate, identify, match meaning, retrieve without options; teach model sentence, contextual gap/reorder only when useful; then listen and respond, novel real-world task and delayed retrieval. Vary the exercise mix with the goal. The 15-function contract and 24 UI concepts are a pool, not a mandatory template.
- Early literacy/phonology runs alongside communication. Grapheme/name/sound, auditory contrast, whole-word pronunciation, meaning, connected phrases; Czech vowel length, stress, ch, soft consonants, ř, clusters and voicing. Do not copy English phonics rules. Distinguish real minimal pairs from illustrative contrasts. No mandatory long alphabet-only gate.
- Pre-A1 speaking tasks should not default to keyboard. Dictation and writing have a place in explicit literacy goals and later levels. Track listening, meaning, model repetition, independent recall, contextual use, spelling, pronunciation and retention independently. Transcript matching is not acoustic or semantic mastery.
- Review errors after other exercises when possible; then spaced recall. Research05 1/3/7/14/30-day intervals and up to 20% due review are hypotheses, not empirically optimal. Same-day repetition must not inflate spaced mastery.
## Initial outcome candidates (not locked syllabus)
C01 greet/leave appropriately; C02 self-introduction with own name; C03 exchange names; C04 accept/decline politely; C05 repair misunderstanding; C06 ask for help; C07 brief social exchange; C08 essential personal details; C09 practical numbers; C10 shopping. Review the sequence against Czech level descriptions and real communicative needs. Do not extend the old L04–L18 draft by inertia. Three revised pilot lessons are candidate inputs, not canonical approved lessons.

## Scale gate
Complete one small speaking pilot with linguistically checked CZ/MN, approved playable audio, actual UI/device evidence, independent response and delayed recall evidence. If unavailable mark pending. No bulk content, no fixed target count, no false release approval.
