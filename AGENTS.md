## One active workstream
- Work on main only. Never create a new branch, repo, prototype app, standalone HTML app, or separate learning engine just because a new chat begins. An exception requires explicit owner approval.
- Do not continue or merge old PRs wholesale. Historical branches are reference-only; do not delete them or rewrite their history. Selectively recover useful files after checking compatibility.
- Read the current PROJECT_STATE before work. Complete the single NEXT task or record its actual blocker. Do not parallel-edit the same files from multiple chats. Re-read HEAD before commit; use a non-force fast-forward update, preferably an expected-head lease. If HEAD moves, stop and reconcile, not overwrite.
- Small atomic commits; validate changes before pushing executable code. Preserve functioning app routes, reader, cards, dictionary and AI integration. Do not push unreviewed public learning content or trigger paid work without authorization.
- After each completed work item update PROJECT_STATE with evidence (commit, tests, limitations, next action), not a new roadmap. Do not mark work complete from a plan, mock test or screenshot alone.
## Pedagogy and QA
- Speaking and listening in real situations are the priority. Curriculum determines UI and code; never force content into legacy 72/140/18 counts or fixed step counts.
- Use evidence, record source/version/license. Unknown corpus rank is null. Separate authored examples from corpus observations. Teach required forms before testing; separate recognition, recall, pronunciation, and independent communication.
- Run automated QA yourself; owner may listen and approve or use app, not perform developer QA. Mock STT/TTS tests verify flow only. Do not claim native Czech validation, real Safari, or pronunciation grading without evidence.
- No private Drive IDs, audio archives, credentials, tokens or signed URLs in this public repo. Preserve existing audio, resolve approved clips before generation, no new cost or public release without approval.
